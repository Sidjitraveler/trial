faq

stuff people ask.

do I need to prep anything?

Just a clear space of about 7 × 5 ft. A living room or bedroom is perfect. I bring everything else, including towels and sheets. A shower beforehand is nice but not required.

do you see women clients?

Yes. I see clients of all genders, with the same professional draping and boundaries for everyone. You're welcome to have someone else at home during the session.

how do I pay?

By UPI or cash after the session. For first-time bookings very early or late in the day, I might ask for a small advance to hold the slot.

what if I need to cancel?

It's free if you cancel at least 3 hours before. Later cancellations may cost ₹500, because I've usually turned someone else away for that slot.

is massage okay for me?

Tell me beforehand if you're pregnant, recently injured, or have blood-pressure, skin or other medical conditions. If you're not sure, check with your doctor first. Massage doesn't replace medical treatment.
