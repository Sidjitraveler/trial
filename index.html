about

hey there

    I leave, you rest

    Stay lying down while I pack up quietly. You pay the rest by UPI or cash.

    • The room is left exactly as I found it
    • The next day I'll message to ask how you're feeling
    • Honest feedback is always welcome, good or bad

faq

stuff people ask.

do I need to prep anything?

Just a clear space of about 7 × 5 ft. A living room or bedroom is perfect. I bring everything else, including towels and sheets. A shower beforehand is nice but not required.

I'm a woman. will I feel safe?

That's the most important question here, and a fair one to ask. You can have a friend or family member at home during the session. I'm happy to share my ID before I come. You stay draped the whole time, and you can stop at any point, no questions asked.

are you certified?

No, and I'd rather be upfront about that. I'm not a trained or certified therapist. I've had a lot of massages myself and I offer relaxation massage only. For an injury or a medical condition, please see a physiotherapist.

when are you available?

Saturdays and Sundays only, between 7 am and 10 pm. This is part-time for me, so please book at least 3 days in advance. Weekend slots fill up, so earlier is better.

how much does it cost?

₹2,500 per massage (about an hour), plus travel, which depends on how far you are. I'll confirm the total before you book. A ₹500 advance confirms the slot and comes off the total.

how do I pay?

You pay a ₹500 advance by UPI to confirm the booking. The rest, plus the travel charge, is paid by UPI or cash after the session.

what if I need to cancel?

Tell me at least 24 hours before and I'll refund the ₹500 advance or move it to another weekend. If you cancel later than that, I keep the advance, because I've usually turned someone else away for that slot.

is massage okay for me?

If you're pregnant, recently injured, or have blood-pressure, skin or other medical conditions, please check with your doctor first and tell me beforehand. I'm not medically trained, and this doesn't replace treatment.
